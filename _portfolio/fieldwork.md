Field campaigns provide essential constraints for understanding glacier thermodynamics, ice motion, abrupt cryospheric hazards, and high-mountain water resources.

Recent and past expeditions include Bayi Glacier in the Qilian Mountains, Muztagh Glacier in Xinjiang, southeastern Tibetan Plateau glaciers, the 40th Chinese National Antarctic Research Expedition, the 7th Chinese National Arctic Research Expedition, the NEEM deep ice core expedition in Greenland, and glacier campaigns around Mount Everest and the Yarlung Zangbo headwaters.
