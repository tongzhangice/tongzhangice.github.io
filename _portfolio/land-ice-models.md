I develop land ice models that connect glacier and ice-sheet mechanics with climate forcing, ocean interactions, geothermal boundary conditions, and hydrological change.

My current model-development work includes the two-dimensional Polythermal Land Ice Model (PoLIM) and the three-dimensional Polar Land Ice Simulator (PoLarIS). These models support studies of mountain glaciers, polar ice sheets, glacier detachment, ice-shelf damage, and long-term sea-level projections.

I have also contributed to the U.S. Department of Energy MPAS-Albany Land Ice Model (MALI), a variable-resolution ice-sheet model designed for Earth system modeling.
