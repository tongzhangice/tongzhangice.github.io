--- title: "Cryosphere-Climate Interactions" excerpt: "Ice-sheet instability, sea-level rise, glacier runoff, and abrupt cryosphere events." collection: portfolio --- My research examines how land ice responds to atmospheric and oceanic forcing, and how those responses affect sea level, regional water resources, and climate-risk assessment. Key topics include Antarctic and Greenland ice-sheet instability, basal melting beneath Antarctic ice shelves, geothermal heat flow beneath the Greenland ice sheet, glacier runoff timing around Mount Everest, and abrupt glacier detachment processes in the southeastern Tibetan Plateau. This work links numerical modeling, field evidence, remote sensing observations, and international model intercomparison projects such as ISMIP6 and ISMIP7.
