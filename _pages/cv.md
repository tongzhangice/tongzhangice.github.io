Education
======
* 2010-2013: Ph.D. in Physical Geography, Cold and Arid Regions Environmental and Engineering Research Institute, Chinese Academy of Sciences
* 2007-2010: M.S. in Physical Geography, Cold and Arid Regions Environmental and Engineering Research Institute, Chinese Academy of Sciences
* 2003-2007: B.S. in Materials Physics, Northwestern Polytechnical University

Work Experience
======
* 2021-present: Associate Professor, Faculty of Geographical Science, Beijing Normal University
* 2017-2020: Postdoctoral Researcher, Solid Mechanics and Fluid Dynamics Group, Los Alamos National Laboratory
* 2015-2017: Assistant Researcher, Institute of Climate System, Chinese Academy of Meteorological Sciences
* 2013-2015: Postdoctoral Researcher, Department of Mathematics, University of South Carolina

Research Interests
======
* Land ice dynamics in the Earth climate system
* Antarctic and Greenland ice-sheet instability and sea-level rise
* Tibetan Plateau glacier change, water resources, and glacier hazards
* Physics-based modeling integrated with artificial intelligence and observations
* Development of two-dimensional and three-dimensional land ice dynamics models

* 2023-2026: National Key R&D Program on Greenland ice-sheet monitoring, modeling, and climate impacts, key participant
* 2022-2025: Third Xinjiang Scientific Expedition Program on cryosphere function services, key participant
* 2022-2024: CAS independent research project on numerical simulation of ice-collapse processes, principal investigator
* 2021-2023: Beijing Normal University State Key Laboratory project on Himalayan glaciers and water-resource change, principal investigator
* 2017-2019: NSFC Young Scientists Fund on three-dimensional Stokes modeling of East Antarctic glacier thermodynamics, principal investigator
