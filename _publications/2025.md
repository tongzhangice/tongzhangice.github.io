Glacier detachment is a severe natural hazard that can cause substantial downstream damage. This study investigates the 2018 Sedongpu Glacier detachment in southeastern Tibet using a two-dimensional first-order ice-flow model with a positive feedback between ice stiffness and basal slip. The simulations show how abrupt weakening during the transition from elastic to plastic deformation can trigger glacier detachment tipping processes.
